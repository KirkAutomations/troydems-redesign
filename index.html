Make a Difference

Get Involved

What will you tell the next generation when they ask what you did to keep America a democracy?

1

Canvass for a Candidate

Walk door-to-door meeting neighbors and sharing campaign literature. Training provided — a great way to get your steps in.

2

Run for Precinct Delegate

Help neighbors understand issues, work on campaigns, and vote on the Democratic Party platform. Many Troy seats are unopposed.

3

Join a City Board

Parks, sustainable design, city planning, historical preservation — every department has advisory committees seeking citizen volunteers.

4

Write Letters to the Editor

Editorial boards listen to their readers. Local letters get more attention — make a list of papers and start writing.

5

Personalize Postcards

Direct mail is proven to sway voters. Handwrite postcards at your own pace to make a difference from your own space.

6

Join a Campaign

Every candidate needs office help, speechwriters, and organizers. If your skills lean toward the written arts, they need you.

7

Phone Bank

Talking to voters is rewarding work. Remind them to vote, make the case for a candidate, or simply answer their questions.

8

Address City Council

City leaders need to hear from citizens. Nearly every council meeting has time for public comments — make your voice heard.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting involved with the Troy Dems.

When and where do you meet?
We meet the fourth Thursday of every month. Most meetings include guest speakers on local and Michigan politics, progressive causes, and community organizing. Request to be added to our email list for monthly invitations with location details.
Do I need to be a registered Democrat to attend?
Everyone is welcome at our meetings. Whether you are a lifelong Democrat, newly interested in politics, or just curious about what we do, we encourage you to attend and get involved.
Where do membership funds go?
Your membership helps fund club activities, outreach programs, and special events. Funds do not go to candidates or elected officials — they support the club’s community engagement and educational mission.
How can young people get involved?
We actively welcome Millennials and Gen Z members. The party is fighting for climate action, reproductive rights, LGBTQI+ protections, and healthcare — issues that directly shape your future. Attend a meeting, volunteer for a campaign, or join our social media communities.
Can I volunteer without attending meetings?
Many volunteer opportunities do not require meeting attendance. Writing letters, personalizing postcards, phone banking, and joining a campaign can all be done on your own schedule from home.
How do I stay updated on events?
Join our official TDC Facebook group for the most up-to-date news and event information. You can also sign up for our email list using the form below to receive monthly meeting invitations and announcements.
